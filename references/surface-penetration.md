# Surface penetration & layered geometry (V6)

Mandatory for every new model and geometry revision; retain all V5 checks. Z-fighting (nearly coplanar faces) differs from a solid crossing a sloped decorative surface. Never fix either by moving every cube by a uniform epsilon. Do not hide geometry errors with texture.

1. Design supporting volumes, then armor, leaves and crystal settings together. Decide each cube's functional role, support bone, intended contact faces and protected outward faces before UV. Identify decorative surfaces that must remain readable. A plate's underside may embed; its upper face must not be breached by its support. For an inlaid gem, the rear and embedded perimeter may be obscured by its socket; front and visible rim are the focal surfaces. A flower's root or a spike may occupy a small specifically designed patch. Record the named exceptions and visually check their extent; never blanket-allow the whole body.
2. For rotated plates inspect all vertices and section views. Reduce unnecessary layers, redesign supporting body into stepped volumes, align plates to support faces, or bake shallow moss, fur, patterns and scratches into UV. Preserve necessary silhouette features. Do not add an overlay cube solely to increase side texture detail. Check attachment contact after moving parts, including parent bones and pivot.
3. Run the all-face rest inspection and explicitly protect the relevant surfaces in a JSON policy, e.g. `{"plate_left":{"protected_faces":["up"],"allow":{"up":["thorn_root"]},"detail_faces":["up","north","south","east","west"]}}`. Use actual cube names (unique). An empty or incomplete policy is not approval. `check_interpenetration.py` clips a world face to the interior of the other transformed solid, reporting pairwise covered area fraction. It handles rotations and affine scale via the inherited numeric-linear transform evaluator. Reports can include intentional, hidden structural overlap; full coverage is not by itself proof that an element is removable. Evaluate all rest candidates; run animated protected-face sampling at 20 Hz plus keyframes. For different-bone joints also inspect all moving parts visually and add protected faces as needed. No assertion that unconfigured animated surfaces were tested.
4. Run `check_attachment.py` with a separate JSON mapping attachment names to acceptable supporting cube names. SAT detects separated boxes, not a full physical contact simulation. Use intended direct supports, and inspect contact patches visually; a contact with an unintended neighbor is not sufficient. Check throughout relevant animation. The sample tolerance 0.08 unit is a review threshold, not an engine guarantee.
5. Geometry changes require new UV sizes if local dimensions change, repack with 1px edge padding, retain IDs, pivots and outliner where possible. Re-run V5 z-fighting, UV, shape and V6 checks, then same-camera before/after rendered views and GIFs. Preserve old animations unless changes are necessary and agreed. Record remaining candidates, policy scope, exceptions and unsupported tracks. Do not claim zero interpenetration from zero configured protected-face hits. Real Blockbench / ModelEngine playback required to claim engine-level resolution.
