# Frost Witch V3.1 change notes

## Applied fixes
- Improved lantern/core texture readability: clearer metal-vs-glow separation, softer glow chamber, less chunky braces.
- Removed the rear square support cube (`staff_shroud`) that caused an awkward box silhouette behind the weapon.
- Tightened the lantern side inlays so they read as frame accents instead of detached blocks in 3/4 view.

## Skill-side guidance added
- Focal prop front textures must separate material groups clearly (frame, core, glow, braces).
- Do not add unsupported rear cubes behind a focal prop unless they read as a deliberate structure from side and back views.
- Rear silhouettes of staffs/lanterns must be reviewed in front, 3/4, side, and back views before finalizing.
- Shallow prop details should default to texture/UV treatment, not thick cubes.
